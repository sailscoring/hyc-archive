# Clarifications

Known divergences between a Sail Scoring reconstruction and HYC's original
published results — things that come down to a modelling decision or a gap in
the captured data, not a Sail Scoring bug.

**The rule:** when a reconstruction can't reproduce the original exactly, record
it here *and* make the published reconstruction carry a note listing the delta
with a link to the original results. We do not silently ship an approximation —
the "good enough" call on the IODAI archive is why this convention exists.

Each entry should capture: which series/season/fleet, what differs, the most
likely cause (HYC/Sailwave modelling choice, missing source data, rounding,
etc.), and a link to the original published page under `sources/reshyc/`.
